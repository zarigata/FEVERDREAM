EZpenguin

A lighter starting point for your own infrastructure.

A minimal Debian-based Linux OS for homelabs, home servers, and self-hosted projects. Start small, choose your software, and keep control of your machine.

Visit the website ↗View repository ↗

Linux · Debian · Self-hosting

  • Debian 12 foundation with an automated first-boot wizard.
  • A 61-entry software catalog for server, development, and everyday administration tasks.
  • amd64 images and ARM64 options for compatible UEFI systems.

Project status

An early-stage project. Broader hardware testing, signing, and hardening remain on the roadmap.
